M# My Favorite Series

## How I Met Your Mother

My absolute favorite series is **How I Met Your Mother**. It is a sitcom that ran for nine seasons.

### Why I love it:
* **The Humor:** The jokes and running gags (like the slap bet) are legendary.
* **The Characters:** Ted, Barney, Robin, Marshall, and Lily have great chemistry.
* **The Storytelling:** I love how the show uses flashbacks and flash-forwards to tell the story.

You can find more information about the show on [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0460649/).
